## A pattern that shows up everywhere

Notice what these have in common:

1. **Real material goes in.** Pasted, attached, or pulled via a connector. Not described.
2. **The output has a clear shape.** A table, a draft, a checklist, a memo — not "thoughts about."
3. **Claude does the heavy reading.** You do the deciding.
4. **You verify the parts that matter.** Especially numbers, names, dates, and any cited source.

If your use case follows that shape, it'll probably work. If it doesn't, that's usually where things slip.
