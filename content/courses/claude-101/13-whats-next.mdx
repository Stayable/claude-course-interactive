# What's next?

You now have the toolkit: what Claude is, how to talk to it, where it lives, what makes prompts work, how Projects and artifacts and skills and connectors fit together, and what good use cases look like across different jobs.

The natural next step depends on what *you* want to do with it. Pick the path that fits.

## If you want to use Claude better at work

You're done with the foundations. Go practice for two weeks before another course. Set yourself one rule: every time you'd normally open a search tab or a blank document, try Claude first. You'll learn more from a fortnight of real use than from any course.

When you do come back for more, the natural next step is **AI Fluency: Framework & Foundations** — a deeper take on how to collaborate with AI well, ethically, and safely.

## If you write software

The next course is **Claude Code 101**. It moves you from "Claude in a chat window" to "Claude in your terminal, working on your codebase." Same model, very different surface — and the surface is where the leverage shows up for developers.

From there: **Claude Code in Action** for deeper workflows, then **Introduction to agent skills** and **Introduction to subagents** when you want to package and parallelize your work.

## If you're building a product on top of Claude

You want **Building with the Claude API** — comprehensive, 8 hours of video. It covers the full surface: requests, streaming, tool use, batching, files, citations, prompt caching. There are also platform-specific variants if you're on **Amazon Bedrock** or **Google Vertex AI**.

After the API course, **Introduction to Model Context Protocol** opens up the connector side: building tools Claude can call.

## If you teach, or you work in a school

Three courses are tailored to you:

- **AI Fluency for Educators** — applying the framework into your teaching practice.
- **AI Fluency for Students** — for the people sitting in front of you.
- **Teaching AI Fluency** — for instructor-led settings, with assessment.

## If you run a nonprofit or a small business

**AI Fluency for Nonprofits** or **AI Fluency for Small Businesses** are the right starts — same framework, examples from your world.

## One last habit

The single best thing you can do after this course is make Claude a *first move*, not a *last resort*. Don't save it for "AI tasks." Use it for the small stuff — rewording a sentence, sorting a list, double-checking a sum, drafting the first version of anything. The intuition for when it helps, and when it doesn't, only comes from reps.
