# Enterprise search

Most of what your company "knows" isn't in one place. It's scattered across Drive, SharePoint, Notion, Slack, Confluence, Salesforce, the wiki nobody updates anymore, and an email thread from 2023. **Enterprise search** lets Claude look across all of that in one ask.

It's not magic. It's the same connector idea from the last lesson, but pointed at *the union* of the systems your team uses.

## What it's good at

- **Pulling threads together.** A question whose answer lives partly in a doc, partly in a thread, and partly in someone's notes.
- **"Where is…?"** *Where's the latest version of the security questionnaire? Where did we land on the pricing change?*
- **Onboarding context.** *"What does my team mean when they say 'tier-2 escalation'? Show me prior examples."*
- **Pre-meeting prep.** *"Summarize everything we know about the Northwind account."*

## What it's not

## A small rule for asking

Treat the question the way you'd treat one to a smart new hire: *be specific about scope and time*. "What did Sales decide about discounts?" is too vague. "What did Sales decide about new-logo discounts in Q3?" gives Claude something to actually search for.

<Callout kind="tip">
Always check the cited sources. Enterprise search shows you where each piece of the answer came from — click through when the stakes matter.
</Callout>
