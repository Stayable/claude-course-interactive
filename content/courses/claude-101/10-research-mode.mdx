# Research mode for deep dives

A normal Claude reply takes a few seconds. **Research mode** takes minutes. It's a different gear — for questions where the right answer requires Claude to look in many places, follow leads, and *iterate* on its own findings before answering you.

Use it when the question is too big for one round-trip.

## What you get back

A research run usually ends with three things:

1. **A short answer** — Claude's best read of the question, up front.
2. **The body** — the reasoning, organized into sections.
3. **Sources** — links to every page, doc, or thread that fed into the answer.

The sources are the most important part. They let you check Claude's work and dig further on the parts that matter to *you*, not just the parts Claude found interesting.

## Asking research-mode questions well

A short framing list:

- **State the question once, plainly.** No need to oversell it.
- **Say what you'll *do* with the answer.** "I'm comparing options for a build-vs-buy decision next week." That shapes the depth and the structure.
- **Bound the scope.** Time window, geography, segment — whatever applies. "U.S. only, last 12 months."
- **Say what you already know.** Saves Claude from re-discovering the basics.
